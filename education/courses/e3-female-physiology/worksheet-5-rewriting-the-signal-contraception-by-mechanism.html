Where education ends
This worksheet builds mechanism literacy, not a license to diagnose, prescribe, or recommend a method for yourself or anyone else. Explain how each mechanism works, and treat method selection, side-effect management, and candidacy questions as belonging to a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-prescribe.
