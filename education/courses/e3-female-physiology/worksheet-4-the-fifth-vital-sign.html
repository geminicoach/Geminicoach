Where education ends
This worksheet builds cycle literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the axis works, and treat anything that looks clinical (a cycle that stops for months, bleeding at the extremes, or a sustained departure from someone's own established pattern) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
