Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about energy availability and RED-S, not a license to assess, diagnose, or treat, in yourself or anyone else. A quiet cycle, persistent low energy availability, or any sign of disordered eating are not situations to coach around or wait out. Each one warrants evaluation by a qualified clinician. Recognize the pattern, then refer it.
