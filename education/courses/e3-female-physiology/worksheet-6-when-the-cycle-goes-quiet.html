Where education ends
This worksheet builds recognition skills, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Learn to notice that a quiet cycle outside a clearly expected medically induced change may signal something worth professional evaluation, and stop there. Evaluation, diagnosis, and treatment belong to a qualified clinician. A coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
