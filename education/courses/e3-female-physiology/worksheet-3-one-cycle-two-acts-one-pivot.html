Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of the cycle, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the follicular phase, the pivot, and the luteal phase work, and treat anything that looks clinical, a very long or absent cycle, especially alongside heavy training and reduced eating, as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
