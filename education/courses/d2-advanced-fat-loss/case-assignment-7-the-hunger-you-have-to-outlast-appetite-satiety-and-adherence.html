Chapter 7: Reason Through a Real Adherence Struggle

Take the physiology of appetite and setpoint defense and use it to explain a real struggle, in plain language, within the bounds of general education.

Name: ____________________________ Date: __________ Est. time: 25 min

The Scenario

Scenario · Priya Anand, 30
Priya is training for her first half-marathon while also working through a fat-loss phase, sixteen weeks in. She has dropped from around twenty-five percent to an estimated seventeen percent body fat. Her training volume and her sleep, roughly eight hours a night, have both stayed consistent the entire time. In the last three weeks she describes her hunger as "loud," especially in the evening. She has started eyeing extra portions at dinner, and twice she quietly ate a second dinner without logging it, then felt a flash of shame afterward. She wonders whether she has simply lost her discipline, and she asks you to help her understand what is actually going on.

Your Tasks

  1. Explain, mechanistically, what has changed in Priya's appetite signaling as she has moved from twenty-five percent toward seventeen percent body fat.
  2. Explain why her evening hunger specifically has intensified, connecting this to the daily hunger curve and meal structure.
  3. Identify the one or two honest levers most likely to help her specific situation right now, and justify the choice using her details (already-good sleep, already-consistent training).
  4. Decide whether her described pattern (loud hunger, two unlogged second dinners, followed by shame) reads as expected setpoint-defense hunger or as a sign that warrants professional evaluation. Explain what specific features would push it toward the latter, and what keeps it, as described, within ordinary physiology.
  5. State clearly where general education ends and clinical referral begins for a situation like this one.

Where education ends
You are demonstrating understanding and plain-language communication, not diagnosing Priya or anyone else. A responsible response explains the mechanism, identifies what if anything warrants professional evaluation, and does not diagnose. Diagnosis and treatment belong to a qualified clinician.
Instructor / self-check rubric
  • Explains the leptin fall and ghrelin rise driving Priya's escalating hunger as her body fat has dropped, and names this as setpoint defense rather than a discipline failure.
  • Explains why evening hunger specifically intensifies (ghrelin build across the day, the daily hunger curve, meal structure).
  • Selects and justifies levers specific to her profile: her sleep and training are already solid, so the highest-yield remaining levers are a higher per-meal protein dose and, given her leanness, a planned diet break.
  • Correctly reasons that her described pattern sits within ordinary physiology as stated, while accurately naming what would push it toward a sign warranting evaluation (persistent loss of control, escalating secrecy, distress that dominates her relationship with food).
  • States clearly where general education ends and professional referral begins, and does not diagnose Priya with anything.
