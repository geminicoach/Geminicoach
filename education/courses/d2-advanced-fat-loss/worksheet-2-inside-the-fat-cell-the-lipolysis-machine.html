Where education ends
This worksheet builds mechanistic literacy about fat mobilization, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the cascade works, and treat any sudden change in fat distribution, new pain, or swelling as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
