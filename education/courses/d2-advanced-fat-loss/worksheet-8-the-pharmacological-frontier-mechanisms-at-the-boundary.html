Where education ends
This worksheet builds pharmacological literacy: naming mechanisms and intended benefits, not a license to dose, source, or recommend the use of any compound, in yourself or anyone else. Decisions about pharmacology belong to a qualified prescribing clinician operating inside a monitored medical relationship. Work within your role: an educator explains mechanism and refers, a clinician prescribes and monitors, and a self-learner does not self-prescribe.
