Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy in appetite physiology, not a license to diagnose or treat disordered eating, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat persistent hunger dysregulation, loss of control around food, or significant distress as signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: an educator explains and refers, a clinician diagnoses and treats, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
