Where education ends
This worksheet builds the judgment to grade a supplement claim against physiology, not a license to diagnose, prescribe, or design a stack. Explain the mechanism, and treat anything that looks clinical (elevated heart rate, palpitations, disrupted sleep, anxiety, or any cardiovascular symptom) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
