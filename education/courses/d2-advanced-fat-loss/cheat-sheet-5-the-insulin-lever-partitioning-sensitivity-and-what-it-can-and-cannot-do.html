Chapter 5 : The Insulin Lever

A storage signal and a brake on fat release, not a villain and not a magic switch.

What Insulin Actually Does

Glucose uptakeMoves GLUT4 to the cell surface in muscle and fat; skeletal muscle handles up to 80% of insulin-stimulated glucose disposal.
Directing glucoseFavors glycogen storage in muscle; the direct carbohydrate-to-fat pathway is quantitatively modest on a typical mixed diet.
Trapping fatPromotes esterification and triglyceride synthesis, keeping dietary fatty acids out of circulation for a period after a fat-containing meal.
The brakeSuppresses lipolysis by lowering cyclic AMP, the direct counterweight to catecholamine drive. This is the action that matters most for fat loss.

Levels vs Sensitivity

  • Insulin levels: how much hormone is circulating right now.
  • Insulin sensitivity: how effectively tissue responds to a given amount.
  • Sensitivity is the more useful concept: it is a tissue property, it responds to honest levers, and it explains why two people handle the same meal differently.

The Four Honest Levers

  • Exercise: acute, insulin-independent GLUT4 recruitment plus chronic capacity gains (Collins et al., 2022).
  • Muscle mass: the body's largest glucose sink; benefit runs alongside training, not from size alone (Paquin et al., 2021).
  • Sleep: partial restriction degrades glucose tolerance within days and raises hunger (Spiegel et al., 2005).
  • Fat loss itself: improves sensitivity chiefly by lowering fatty acid mobilization, not by boosting fat-burning capacity (Schenk et al., 2009).

Why Resistance Happens

  • Chronic energy surplus drives ectopic lipid into liver and muscle, which impairs insulin signaling (Samuel & Shulman, 2016).
  • Muscle resistance appears first, diverting glucose toward the liver and new fat synthesis.
  • Adipose tissue insulin resistance is best defined as excess free fatty acid flux (Carpentier, 2021).
  • The arrow runs from surplus to resistance, not from insulin to obesity.

Retire This Myth

  • The strong carbohydrate-insulin model has been experimentally falsified in controlled feeding trials (Hall, 2017).
  • Controlling insulin does not drive fat loss independent of energy balance.
  • Population fit is decisive: this lever matters most for higher body fat, less sensitive profiles, and offers almost nothing to a lean, already-sensitive athlete.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of insulin's real role, not a license to diagnose or treat. An abnormal lab result, unusual fatigue, excessive thirst, or unexplained weight change are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
