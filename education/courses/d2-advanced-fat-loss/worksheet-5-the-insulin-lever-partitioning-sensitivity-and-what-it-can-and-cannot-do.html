Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of how insulin and sensitivity work, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain the mechanism, and treat anything that looks clinical (an abnormal lab result, unusual fatigue, excessive thirst, or unexplained weight change) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
