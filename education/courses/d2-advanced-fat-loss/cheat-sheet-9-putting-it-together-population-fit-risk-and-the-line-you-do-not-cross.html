Chapter 9 : Putting It Together

One framework, three axes, and a boundary that never moves.

The Three Axes

Population fitIs this person's baseline physiology and life circumstances a plausible match for this tool at all? Checked through training age, recovery capacity, adherence history, and psychological relationship to tracking and body image.
Honest effect sizeHow much does the tool actually move the needle, expressed as a magnitude with a range, not a binary "it works." Significance is not the same as size.
RiskWhat can this cost, and to whom, if it goes wrong: physiological, opportunity, financial, psychological, and scope cost. Risk is asymmetric and stacks across a whole program.

The Mechanisms Behind the Axes

  • Fat cell : beta-adrenergic acceleration vs. alpha-2 braking; the real physiology behind "stubborn" fat.
  • Mitochondrion : oxidative capacity sets the ceiling on how much released fuel is actually burned.
  • Exercise modality : HIIT, steady-state, and resistance training each carry a distinct signature and recovery cost.
  • Insulin lever : governs nutrient partitioning; benefit hits a ceiling once normal sensitivity is reached.
  • Supplement shelf : mechanism determines honest effect size, not the label.
  • Adherence physiology : leptin and ghrelin defend body weight; adherence is engineered, not demanded.
  • Advanced pharmacology : large effect size, strictly clinical territory.

The Claim Autopsy

  • 1. Locate the mechanism on the master map. No plausible mechanism is itself a grade.
  • 2. Estimate honest effect size and variability; rate the certainty of the evidence.
  • 3. Identify the best-fit population. "Works" always means "works for whom."
  • 4. Rate risk and scope. Scope is a gate, not a slider to trade against a strong effect size.

Key Findings to Remember

  • Cifuentes et al. (2023) : phenotype-matched intervention, 7.4kg vs. 4.3kg loss over 12 weeks. Fit was the difference, not a harsher protocol.
  • Mittendorfer et al. (2023) : baseline insulin action determined metabolic payoff; benefit hit a ceiling.
  • Schober et al. (2018) : a p-value says nothing about the size of an effect.
  • Lemstra et al. (2016) : early results and supervised support predict adherence.

The Permanent Boundary

  • Coaching optimizes training, nutrition, sleep, and behavior. It does not prescribe, diagnose, or treat.
  • Every symptom, pain, or dysfunction is a sign that routes to professional evaluation, never a finding to interpret yourself.
  • A tool can clear all three axes and still be out of scope; scope sits before the axes, not inside them.
Where education ends   This builds a decision-making framework, not a license to prescribe, diagnose, or treat, in yourself or anyone else. When a situation calls for diagnosis or treatment, that belongs to a qualified clinician.
