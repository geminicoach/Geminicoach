The Scenario

Scenario · Owen Vance, 36
Owen is a warehouse supervisor, roughly twenty-nine percent body fat, largely sedentary outside of work, with two prior weight-loss attempts that both ended in regain. He carries moderate stress and sleeps about six and a half hours most weeknights. He has read about a continuous glucose monitor and a "thermogenic fat-burner" stack online and wants to know whether he should add either one to a plan. He also mentions that a coworker takes a prescription GLP-1 medication and asks whether he should just ask his own doctor about that instead of "doing all this work."

Your Tasks

  1. Score Owen's plausible fit for the fundamentals (a sustainable deficit, daily movement, resistance training, sleep) versus the continuous glucose monitor and the thermogenic stack, and explain your reasoning.
  2. Evaluate the honest effect size of the continuous glucose monitor and the thermogenic stack specifically for someone in Owen's situation, who is not diabetic and is early in the process.
  3. Evaluate risk for each option, including the psychological and adherence-history risk given his two prior failed attempts.
  4. Produce a ranked recommendation: which tool or tools, if any, earn a place in Owen's plan right now, and in what order.
  5. Address Owen's question about asking his doctor about a prescription GLP-1 medication. Is that within his scope of action? What is your scope as an educator regarding that medication?

Where education ends
You are demonstrating the three-axis framework, not prescribing, diagnosing, or managing anything for Owen. Coaching optimizes training, nutrition, sleep, and behavior. Any question about medication belongs to Owen and his own prescribing clinician.

  • Correctly weighs risk for each tool, including psychological and adherence-history risk given his two prior failed attempts.
