Chapter 6 : Grading the Thermogenic Shelf

Every popular compound touches one of four cascade points. Grade the step, not the label. No dosing, no stacks.

The Four Intervention Points

1. Raise the signalCaffeine: antagonizes adenosine receptors, modestly inhibits phosphodiesterase. SMD around 0.65 to 0.73 for fat oxidation, largely attenuated in trained and habitual users.
2. Release the brakeYohimbine: alpha-2 antagonist. Effective in lean, fasted, sympathetically-driven populations with stubborn alpha-2-rich depots; suppressed after a meal; negligible fit in sedentary or higher body-fat populations.
3. Slow the off-switchGreen-tea catechins (EGCG): inhibit COMT. Small pooled effect (about 1.31 kg), diminished in habitual high-caffeine users.
4. Sensory route inCapsaicinoids: activate TRPV1. Small thermogenic effect, possibly more useful appetite effect, fades with habitual spice exposure.

The Grading Rubric

  • 1. Which exact step does it touch?
  • 2. What is the honest effect size?
  • 3. How reliable is that effect?
  • 4. Who is it for, and who should skip it?

The Receptor Tug-of-War

  • Beta-adrenergic: the accelerator on lipolysis.
  • Alpha-2 adrenergic: the brake on lipolysis.
  • Stubborn depots are alpha-2-rich and beta-poor; "stubborn fat" is a receptor ratio, not a metaphor.

Synephrine, Honestly

  • Weak adrenergic agonist; at rest, changes neither energy expenditure nor fat oxidation versus placebo.
  • During exercise, shifts the fuel mix modestly toward fat without raising total energy expenditure.
  • Resting thermogenic claims for blends are most likely attributable to co-ingredient caffeine.

The Stimulant Ledger

  • Benefit is sub-additive: overlapping mechanisms crowd the same signaling space.
  • Cardiovascular and sympathetic cost is close to fully additive: heart rate and blood pressure do not know which molecule raised them.
  • Any cardiovascular symptom, palpitations, or disrupted sleep is a reason to route to a clinician, not to self-titrate.
Where education ends   This builds judgment to grade a compound against physiology, not a license to diagnose, prescribe, or design a stack. Elevated heart rate, palpitations, disrupted sleep, anxiety, or any cardiovascular symptom are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
