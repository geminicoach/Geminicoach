Where education ends
This worksheet builds fat-loss literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the physiology works, and treat anything that reads as genuine dysfunction, such as persistent fatigue that does not lift with sleep, menstrual disruption, marked mood change, or disordered-eating signals, as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
