Where education ends
This worksheet builds mechanistic literacy about thermogenesis, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the tissues and pathways work, and treat chest discomfort, fainting, extreme cold intolerance, or dizziness that does not resolve quickly as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
