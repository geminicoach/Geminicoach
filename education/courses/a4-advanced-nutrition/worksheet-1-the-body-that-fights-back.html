Where education ends
This worksheet builds metabolic literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system defends its fuel, and treat anything that looks clinical (signs of an eating disorder, missing periods, hair loss, extreme fatigue, medication questions, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
