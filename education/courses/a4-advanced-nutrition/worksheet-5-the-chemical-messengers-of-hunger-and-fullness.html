Part D · The Control Room and the Two Appetites

Where education ends
This worksheet builds nutrition literacy, not a license to diagnose, treat, or prescribe, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the signaling system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (a possible thyroid disorder, a request to start or dose a medication, or alarm signs such as unexplained rapid weight loss) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. GLP-1 and other weight-loss medications are prescribed and managed by clinicians, never through this course. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
