Chapter 5 : The Chemical Messengers of Hunger and Fullness

An information channel between body and brain: hormones report energy status upward, and the hypothalamus tunes hunger, satiety, and burn.

The Two Clocks

  • Leptin : from fat tissue; roughly proportional to fat stores. The slow, long-term fuel gauge. High leptin permits normal appetite and expenditure; a fall reads as a threat.
  • Ghrelin : from the stomach; rises before a habitual meal, drops after eating. The fast, hour-to-hour hunger alarm.
  • The two operate largely independently. Not one seesaw, but two separate instruments.

Insulin, Rehabilitated

  • Governs the timing of nutrient storage and release, not the total fat you carry.
  • Fat can be stored without dietary carbohydrate or elevated insulin; fat balance tracks total energy.
  • The strong carbohydrate-insulin model fails under controlled feeding studies.
  • Centrally, insulin is a satiety signal: it reports "food has arrived" and reduces intake.

The Satiety Committee

  • Ghrelin : starts the meal; falls once food arrives.
  • CCK : upper small intestine, within minutes; slows gastric emptying, signals via the vagus nerve.
  • PYY : lower gut; sustains fullness over the next hour or two.
  • GLP-1 : lower gut; slows emptying, sharpens insulin; the target of the medications.
  • Insulin : also a central "enough" signal.

The Control Room

  • Signals converge on the arcuate nucleus of the hypothalamus.
  • AgRP / NPY neurons drive hunger; POMC neurons drive satiety. They compete.
  • Ghrelin tips it toward hunger; leptin and insulin toward satiety.
  • A sustained deficit lowers leptin, so hunger gets louder and satiety quieter.

Two Appetites

  • Homeostatic : tracks energy need; defends fat stores.
  • Hedonic : reward-based; can override fullness for highly palatable food.
  • You can be physically full and still want dessert: two systems, two currencies.
  • Engineered energy-dense food is built to win that contest. Manage the environment, do not just resist harder.

Eating With the Signals

  • Protein : most satiating macronutrient; triggers gut peptides, high thermic effect, spares lean mass.
  • Volume : the reliable lever; stomach stretch reports fullness on physical bulk, not calories.
  • Fiber : helps selectively (for example viscous fibers), not as a universal switch.
  • Sleep & stress : short sleep cuts leptin ~18% and raises ghrelin ~28%; stress pushes intake up.

Adaptive Thermogenesis & GLP-1 Medications

Adaptive thermogenesisDuring weight loss, falling leptin, lower sympathetic activity, and reduced T3 nudge expenditure down (about 120 kcal/day on average, wide variation). The body defending its fat, not a broken metabolism.
Thyroid (T3/T4)Sets metabolic rate and acts centrally on appetite via the arcuate nucleus. Its dip during dieting is part of the same defensive response. Genuine thyroid disorder is a medical matter.
GLP-1 agonistsSemaglutide, liraglutide: engineered to resist breakdown so a short-lived satiety signal stays active for hours or days. People report food is less interesting and fullness lasts longer. Prescribed and managed by clinicians only.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of how the appetite-signaling system works, not a license to diagnose, treat, or prescribe. Read hunger as a signal to interpret, never as a failure of willpower to punish. GLP-1 and other weight-loss medications, and any suspected thyroid disorder, are decisions for a qualified clinician, not for this course. Work within your role, whatever it is.
