Part C · Speed, Drift, and the Fiber Gap

Two facts about the microbiome pull in opposite directions. Reason through both.

  1. David and colleagues (2014) showed the community shifts within about a day of a new diet, then drifts back toward baseline when the old diet returns. Use both halves of that result to explain why a one-week "gut reset" cannot buy a permanent change, and what the community is actually tracking.

Where education ends
This worksheet builds nutrition literacy, not a license to diagnose, treat, or prescribe, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the ecosystem works, and treat anything that looks clinical (persistent diarrhea, blood in the stool, unexplained weight loss, a suspected inflammatory bowel or celiac condition, or a request to start a probiotic protocol or a fecal transplant) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Fecal microbiota transplantation is a regulated medical treatment for a narrow indication, never a lifestyle service offered through this course. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
