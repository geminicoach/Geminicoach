Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy in how the phosphagen system powers brief, explosive effort, not a license to diagnose or treat, or to prescribe individualized supplementation. Treat any question involving a pre-existing medical condition, medication, or organ health as needing a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
