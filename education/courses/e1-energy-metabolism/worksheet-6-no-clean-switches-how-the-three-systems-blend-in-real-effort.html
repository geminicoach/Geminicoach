Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about how the three energy systems blend, not a license to diagnose or treat, or to design an individualized training program. Explain how the systems work together, and treat anything that looks clinical (chest pain, irregular heartbeat, unusual dizziness, or other alarm signs during exercise) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
