Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about energy accounting, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Persistent fatigue, cold intolerance, hair changes, or missed menstrual cycles alongside restricted eating are signs that may reflect thyroid dysfunction, relative energy deficiency, or disordered eating, and they warrant evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional, not self-management from a course. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
