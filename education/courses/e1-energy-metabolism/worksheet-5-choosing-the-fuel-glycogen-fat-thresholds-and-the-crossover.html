Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about fuel selection, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (confusion, unsteadiness, fainting, or a diagnosed metabolic condition) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
