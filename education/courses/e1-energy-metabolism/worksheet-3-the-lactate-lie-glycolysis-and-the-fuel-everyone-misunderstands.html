Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy in glycolysis and lactate metabolism, not a license to diagnose or treat. Ordinary post-exercise soreness and burn are expected physiology, but unusually prolonged soreness, dark urine, severe swelling, or other alarm signs warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician, since they can signal rare but serious muscle injury. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
