Where education ends
This worksheet builds an integrated understanding of energy metabolism, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Persistent unexplained changes in weight, energy, temperature tolerance, or menstrual function, and suspected relative energy deficiency in sport, are clinical questions that belong to a qualified healthcare professional. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
