Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about metabolic flexibility and mitochondrial adaptation, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Thyroid dysfunction, type 2 diabetes, and metabolic syndrome are genuine clinical conditions that require evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional, not self-diagnosis based on how a workout felt. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
