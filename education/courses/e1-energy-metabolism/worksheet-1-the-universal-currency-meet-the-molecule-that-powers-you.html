Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy in energy metabolism, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the ATP system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (unexplained persistent weakness, fatigue that does not track with training, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
