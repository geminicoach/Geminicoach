Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about the aerobic energy system, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (chest pain, unusual breathlessness, fainting, or other alarm signs during exercise) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
