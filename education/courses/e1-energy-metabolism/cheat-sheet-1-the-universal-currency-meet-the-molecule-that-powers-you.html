Chapter 1 : The Universal Currency

One molecule, spent and rebuilt thousands of times a day: ATP is flow, not storage.

The Anatomy of ATP

AdenineA nitrogen-containing ring structure; also one of the letters in the code of deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA).
RiboseA five-carbon sugar; joined to adenine, the two together are called adenosine.
Three phosphatesNegatively charged groups chained onto adenosine; "tri" means three, giving adenosine triphosphate (ATP).
ADP, AMPAdenosine diphosphate (two phosphates) and adenosine monophosphate (one phosphate): ATP's less-charged relatives.

The Hydrolysis Reaction

  • Hydrolysis : water splits the bond to the outer phosphate. ATP + water → ADP + inorganic phosphate (Pi).
  • This reaction is exergonic (releases usable energy) because the products are more stable than the reactants.
  • Three reasons the products are more stable: relief of electrostatic repulsion, resonance stabilization, and better solvation by water.

The Bond Myth, Corrected

  • Wrong: "Energy is stored in the phosphate bond and released when it breaks."
  • Why it is wrong: breaking any bond, by itself, always costs energy; it never releases it.
  • Correct: the overall reaction is exergonic because the products (ADP + Pi) are more stable than ATP, not because the bond itself was a store of energy.
  • "High-energy phosphate bond" is acceptable shorthand for "a bond whose hydrolysis is unusually exergonic," nothing more.

How ATP Does Real Work

  • Hydrolysis is coupled to a job, usually by phosphorylating a protein and driving a shape change, not by releasing a puff of energy.
  • Myosin cross-bridge : ATP binding detaches myosin from actin; hydrolysis re-cocks the head for the next stroke.
  • Sodium-potassium pump : ATP hydrolysis phosphorylates the pump, driving 3 sodium ions out and 2 potassium ions in per cycle.

The Spend-and-Rebuild Cycle

  • Resynthesis : continuously rebuilding ATP from ADP + Pi using energy captured from food.
  • Food is not burned directly for work; food energy pays for resynthesis, then ATP is spent on the job.
  • Energy charge : a 0 to 1.0 gauge of the ATP/ADP/AMP pool; a healthy resting cell holds about 0.85 to 0.95.
  • Rising AMP is sensed by AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) and by phosphofructokinase, switching on resynthesis pathways within seconds.

The Numbers That Make ATP Strange

  • Stored ATP covers only about two seconds of all-out maximal effort.
  • Whole-body ATP turnover runs roughly 100 to 150 moles (about 50 to 75 kilograms) per day, near your own body weight.
  • Each ATP molecule is therefore spent and rebuilt an estimated 1,000 to 2,000 times per day.
  • Carbo-loading builds glycogen (a fuel reserve), not an "ATP tank"; glycogen fuels continuous ATP resynthesis during long efforts.
