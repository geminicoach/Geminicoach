Chapter 3 : The Lactate Lie

Glycolysis, corrected: lactate is a fuel, not a waste product, and it does not cause the burn or next-day soreness.

Glycolysis Basics

What it isThe anaerobic splitting of glucose into two molecules of pyruvate.
ATP yieldNet 2 ATP per glucose molecule (from blood glucose), or net 3 ATP per glucose unit from stored muscle glycogen.
Time frameSlower than the phosphagen system, faster than aerobic metabolism; dominates roughly ten seconds to two minutes of effort.
Switched on byRising AMP, calcium, and epinephrine acting on phosphofructokinase; flux can rise over 100-fold within seconds.

Three Corrections

  • Myth: muscles make lactic acid. Fact: muscles make lactate; the lactate-forming reaction actually consumes a proton, slightly buffering the cell.
  • Myth: lactate is a waste product to clear out. Fact: lactate is a fuel, shuttled to be burned elsewhere or recycled to glucose.
  • Myth: lactate buildup causes next-day soreness. Fact: lactate clears in 30 to 60 minutes; soreness has a completely different, much slower mechanism.

The Lactate Shuttle

  • Roughly 75 to 80 percent of lactate produced is reused, via monocarboxylate transporters.
  • Destinations for reuse as fuel: slow-twitch muscle fibers, the heart, and the brain.
  • The liver recycles lactate back into glucose via the Cori cycle.

What Actually Causes the Burn

  • The real source of exercise acidosis is hydrogen ion accumulation from ATP breakdown itself during high-turnover glycolytic effort.
  • Muscle pH can drop from about 7.0 at rest to roughly 6.4 to 6.8 during maximal glycolytic effort.
  • Lactate and the burn rise together because both stem from the same underlying cause (high ATP turnover), not because one causes the other: correlation, not causation.

DOMS: The Real Mechanism

  • Delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS) traces to eccentric-contraction microdamage, including disruption of the muscle's Z-disks and calcium leakage.
  • An inflammatory repair cascade follows, sensitizing pain receptors.
  • Soreness typically appears one to three days later, long after lactate has already cleared.

Practical Use of Lactate Measures

  • Lactate threshold and maximal lactate steady state are practical tools for pacing and setting training intensity.
  • Lactate concentration is a marker of high metabolic rate, useful for training zones, not the direct cause of fatigue.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of glycolysis and lactate metabolism, not a license to diagnose or treat. Ordinary post-exercise burn and soreness are expected physiology, but unusually severe or prolonged soreness, dark urine, or severe swelling are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
