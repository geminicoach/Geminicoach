Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of the stress response and the recovery brake, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical, a racing heart or palpitations without a clear trigger, chest tightness, shortness of breath, or a wired feeling that never resolves, as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
