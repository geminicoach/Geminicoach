Where education ends
This worksheet builds movement literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how honest ranges of motion work, and treat anything that looks clinical (pain, a sudden change in range, catching or locking, or other signs that warrant professional evaluation) as exactly that. Work within your role, whatever it is: refer what needs referring, and do not diagnose or prescribe treatment.
