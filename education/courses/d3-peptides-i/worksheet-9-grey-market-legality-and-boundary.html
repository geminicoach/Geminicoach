Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about sourcing tiers, contamination risk, and the boundary between legal and safe, not a license to diagnose, treat, or give legal advice. Explain the mechanism and the oversight gap, never a sourcing recommendation, and treat anything that looks clinical (signs of infection, unexplained symptoms after use, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
