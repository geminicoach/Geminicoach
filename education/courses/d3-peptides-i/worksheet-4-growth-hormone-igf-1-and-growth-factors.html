Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of the GH and IGF-1 axis, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (persistent swelling, gradual tissue or skeletal changes, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
