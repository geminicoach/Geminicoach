Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about how the HPG axis is organized and how pattern, not dose, determines its response, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain the mechanism, never a personal protocol, and treat anything that looks clinical (absent cycles, suspected hormonal dysfunction, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
