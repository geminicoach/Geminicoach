Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of the myostatin/ActRIIB pathway, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the mechanism works, and treat anything that looks clinical (unusual bleeding, unexplained vascular changes, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
