Chapter 2 : GH Secretagogues I, GHRH Analogs

One receptor, three engineering paths, and why the pattern of release matters as much as the amount.

The Axis, Step by Step

HypothalamusArcuate nucleus neurons release GHRH into the hypophyseal portal system, a private circulation reaching the pituitary.
SomatostatinA second hypothalamic signal that opposes GHRH, closing the release window and shaping the pulse.
GHRH-RClass B GPCR on the somatotroph; binding activates Gs, adenylate cyclase, cAMP, and PKA.
SomatotrophReleases stored GH within moments, and transcribes new GH over hours.
Liver / IGF-1GH stimulates IGF-1 production; IGF-1's longer half-life makes it a more stable exposure marker.

Pulsatile vs. Tonic

  • Pulsatile : sharp bursts, real troughs, clustered around sleep, exercise, fasting.
  • Tonic : flattened, continuously elevated, no real trough.
  • Continuous stimulation can blunt receptor responsiveness and mute the natural feedback brake.

Three Engineering Paths

  • Sermorelin : GHRH(1-29), unmodified; minutes; closest to a native pulse.
  • Modified GRF (1-29) : DPP-4 resistant substitution; still short and pulse-shaped.
  • CJC-1295 (DAC) : albumin-bound; days-long duration; furthest from a native pulse.
  • Tesamorelin : hexenoic-acid modified full-length backbone; intermediate, once-daily.

Evidence Grade, Compound by Compound

  • Sermorelin : solid trial history, but in children with diagnosed GH deficiency, not healthy adults.
  • CJC-1295 / mod GRF(1-29) : one published human trial (Teichman 2006); thin evidence overall.
  • Tesamorelin : multiple large RCTs; FDA-approved for HIV-associated lipodystrophy specifically.

Known Risks

  • Fluid retention and joint or wrist discomfort, tracking GH/IGF-1 elevation.
  • Reduced insulin sensitivity and rising fasting glucose, a class-wide theoretical concern.
  • IGF-1's growth-promoting, anti-apoptotic signal, an unresolved long-term question under active monitoring.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of the GH axis and its evidence base, not a license to diagnose, treat, or recommend use. Persistent joint swelling, hand or wrist numbness, vision changes, or unexplained fatigue warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
