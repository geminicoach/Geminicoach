Chapter 5: Reason Through an Evidence Claim

Take the myostatin/ActRIIB pathway and use it to evaluate a real claim, in plain language, within the bounds of general education.

Name: ____________________________ Date: __________ Est. time: 20 min

The Scenario

Scenario · Teo, 35
Teo saw the Belgian Blue bull video that opens this chapter, shared alongside the Schuelke (2004) human case report and a vendor listing for an injectable "follistatin 344" product. Teo's reasoning: "If it works this dramatically in cattle, and there's a documented human case with the same gene, then a product built around the same biology is basically proven for people. Why wouldn't I trust it?" Teo has no diagnosed muscle condition and wants an honest read on whether that reasoning holds up.

Your Tasks

  1. Explain the ActRIIB brake mechanism and what removing it actually does at the cellular level (not "unlocking new growth," but removing an existing restraint).
  2. Evaluate Teo's reasoning using the evidence ladder. Where does the cattle, whippet, and human-case evidence actually sit, and how does that differ from the evidence behind the specific vial being sold?
  3. ACE-031, an engineered attempt at the same mechanism, had its most clinically important human trial halted for safety reasons. Explain why, and what that sequence should predict about an unregulated vial claiming the same effect.
  4. Give one practical, general-education takeaway for Teo, and state clearly where general education ends and clinical care begins.

Where education ends
You are demonstrating understanding and evidence-grading skill, not diagnosing or recommending a product. A responsible response explains the mechanism, grades the evidence honestly, and does not provide dosing, cycle, stack, or sourcing guidance. Diagnosis and treatment belong to a qualified clinician.
Instructor / self-check rubric
  • Explains the ActRIIB brake mechanism (Smad2/3, FoxO, breakdown up and synthesis down) accurately.
  • Distinguishes animal and single-case human evidence from human pharmacological evidence, and places the vial correctly, low on the evidence ladder.
  • Explains ACE-031's safety halt (off-target activin A inhibition) as a cautionary parallel for the unverified vial.
  • Notes the mass-versus-strength distinction or the species and evidence-tier specificity of the cited findings.
  • Keeps the response general-education, does not diagnose, and states where general education ends.
