Chapter 7 : The Powerhouse Compounds

Four compounds, one shared target, three entirely different molecules and evidence grades.

The Four Compounds

MOTS-cPeptide (16 amino acids), encoded by mitochondrial DNA itself. Activates AMPK via the folate cycle / purine synthesis, improving insulin sensitivity in animal models. Preclinical only.
Elamipretide (SS-31)Peptide. Binds cardiolipin at the inner membrane cristae, stabilizing the electron transport chain. Positive durable result in Barth syndrome (TAZPOWER); mixed, genotype-dependent result in broader myopathy (MMPOWER-3).
SLU-PP-332Small molecule, NOT a peptide. Synthetic ERR (estrogen-related receptor) agonist, switches on mitochondrial biogenesis genes. Preclinical, no human trial data.
5-amino-1MQSmall molecule. Inhibits NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase), sparing NAD+ and SAM. Preclinical, no human trial data.

Mitochondrion Basics

  • Electron transport chain (ETC) : protein complexes in the inner membrane that produce most of the cell's ATP.
  • Cardiolipin : the distinctive lipid that anchors the ETC and folds the inner membrane into cristae.
  • Oxidative phosphorylation : the ETC-driven process that is far more efficient than glycolysis.
  • Mitochondrial biogenesis : the cell building more mitochondria in response to a signal.

Evidence Status

  • MOTS-c : cell / animal only.
  • SLU-PP-332 : cell / animal only, not a peptide.
  • 5-amino-1MQ : cell / animal only.
  • Elamipretide : real human trials; positive in Barth syndrome, mixed in broader myopathy.
  • Published and preclinical are not the same as clinically studied.

Why "Exercise Mimetic" Needs Care

  • MOTS-c (AMPK) and SLU-PP-332 (ERR) reproduce one downstream signal that exercise also produces.
  • Reproducing one signal is not the same as reproducing everything structured exercise does to a body.
  • Two structurally unrelated molecules converging on overlapping biology is interesting; it is not proof either one substitutes for exercise.

Open Risks and Unknowns

  • Duration : all animal data runs weeks to a few months; chronic-use effects are unstudied.
  • Specificity : ERR and NNMT pathways act on tissue and cofactors used well beyond the intended target.
  • Sourcing : the three non-approved compounds are not manufactured or tested to any pharmaceutical quality standard.
Where education ends   This builds literacy about mitochondrial mechanisms and honest evidence grading, not a license to diagnose or treat. A diagnosed mitochondrial condition, or unexplained, persistent symptoms, are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
