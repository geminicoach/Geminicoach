Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about mitochondrial mechanisms and evidence grading, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how a compound is proposed to work and how far its evidence actually goes, and treat anything that looks clinical (unexplained, persistent symptoms, a diagnosed condition, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
