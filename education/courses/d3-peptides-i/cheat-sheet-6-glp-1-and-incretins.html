Chapter 6 : GLP-1 and Incretins

One gut signal, three coordinated sites of action, and the strongest human evidence base in this entire course.

The Incretin Axis

Meal arrivesNutrients reach the small intestine within minutes; L cells release GLP-1 (broad nutrient trigger), K cells release GIP (fat and carbohydrate).
PancreasGlucose-dependent insulin amplification (the "incretin effect," roughly two to three times the oral vs. IV glucose response) and glucagon suppression.
StomachGastric emptying slows; blunts post-meal glucose rise and prolongs fullness.
BrainHypothalamus and brainstem (including area postrema) reduce appetite; same brainstem region also drives nausea, the class's most common side effect.

Three Drugs, Three Receptor Profiles

  • Semaglutide : single GLP-1 receptor agonist. Approved (Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus). Top of the evidence ladder: large phase 3 RCTs.
  • Tirzepatide : dual GLP-1/GIP agonist. Approved (Mounjaro, Zepbound). Larger average weight loss than semaglutide in trials.
  • Retatrutide : triple GLP-1/GIP/glucagon agonist. Investigational, phase 2 complete, phase 3 underway. One rung down, not unproven.

The Honest Risk Picture

  • GI effects : nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, the leading reason for stopping treatment in trials.
  • Gallbladder / biliary : increased risk, partly confounded by rapid weight loss itself.
  • Lean tissue loss : a real minority share of weight lost is lean mass, not just fat.
  • Thyroid C-cell warning : rodent-specific signal drives a boxed warning and MTC/MEN2 contraindication; large human surveillance data so far reassuring but not decades-long.

Key Terms

  • Incretin effect : oral glucose triggers more insulin than matched IV glucose.
  • DPP-4 : the enzyme that degrades native GLP-1 within one to two minutes; every drug here is engineered to resist it.
  • Molecule vs. vial : trial evidence describes the regulated pharmaceutical product, not an unverified vial sold under the same chemical name.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of GLP-1 and incretin biology, not a license to diagnose or treat. Severe abdominal pain, a relevant personal or family cancer history, or other alarm signs are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
