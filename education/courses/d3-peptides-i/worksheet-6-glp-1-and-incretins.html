Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of GLP-1 and incretin biology, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the mechanism works, and treat anything that looks clinical (severe abdominal pain, a relevant personal or family cancer history, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician.
