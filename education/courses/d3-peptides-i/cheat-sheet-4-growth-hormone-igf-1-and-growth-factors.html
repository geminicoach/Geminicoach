Chapter 4 : Growth Hormone, IGF-1, and Growth Factors

One axis, two tracks (liver-mediated growth, direct metabolic action), and a grey-market family that thins out fast on evidence.

The Two-Track Axis

PituitarySecretes GH in pulses, governed by GHRH (stimulates) and somatostatin (suppresses); largest pulses in deep sleep and after intense exercise.
Liver (indirect track)GH binds the GH receptor (GHR), triggers JAK2/STAT5 signaling, and the liver manufactures and releases systemic IGF-1. Most of GH's growth-promoting reputation runs through this step.
Fat and muscle (direct track)GH also acts directly, independent of IGF-1: raises lipolysis (fat breakdown) and opposes insulin, reducing glucose uptake ("diabetogenic" action).
Systemic IGF-1More than 99% bound and buffered by IGFBPs (mainly IGFBP-3); only the small free fraction can activate a receptor. IGF-1 receptor is structurally close to the insulin receptor.

The Grey-Market Family & Evidence Grade

  • GH itself : approved for defined deficiency; tested in healthy active adults, modest lean mass gain, no strength gain, edema and elevated lactate.
  • Mecasermin (rhIGF-1) : approved only for severe primary IGF-1 deficiency where GHR does not work.
  • IGF-1 LR3 / DES : engineered to escape IGFBP binding; mechanistic and animal evidence only, real hypoglycemia risk.
  • AOD-9604 / frag 176-191 : C-terminal fragment; promising rodent lipolysis data, human obesity trials did not confirm benefit, program discontinued.
  • PEG-MGF : pegylated synthetic MGF (IGF-1Ec); natural MGF is real but local and brief, pegylation defeats that design, no human data on the synthetic product.

Risks of Sustained Excess

  • Insulin resistance : direct track raises blood glucose and reduces glucose uptake.
  • Fluid retention : sodium and water retention; can compress the median nerve (carpal-tunnel-like symptoms).
  • Acromegalic changes : chronic excess drives soft tissue and skeletal overgrowth, joint disease, organ enlargement, cardiovascular and metabolic complications.
  • Cancer-signaling concern : IGF-1 sits upstream of PI3K/AKT and RAS/MAPK; acromegaly correlates with higher cancer incidence, profound lifelong GH/IGF-1 deficiency with lower incidence. Correlational, not proof of individual causation.

Key Terms

  • GHR / JAK2-STAT5 : the liver-side receptor pathway that converts a GH signal into IGF-1 gene transcription.
  • IGFBP-3 : the main circulating buffer protein that keeps most IGF-1 inactive and stable.
  • Pegylation : chemically attaching polyethylene glycol to a peptide to slow its clearance and extend how long it circulates.
  • Evidence ladder : approved and human-tested, above mechanistically plausible but unproven, above preclinical only, above anecdote or marketing.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of GH and IGF-1 biology, not a license to diagnose or treat. Persistent swelling, gradual tissue or skeletal changes, or other alarm signs are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
