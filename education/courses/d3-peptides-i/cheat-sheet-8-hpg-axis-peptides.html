Chapter 8 : The Rhythm Behind the Axis

Three tiers, one feedback loop, and a receptor system that reads pattern before it reads dose.

The Three Tiers

1. HypothalamusKNDy (kisspeptin, neurokinin B, dynorphin) neurons generate the pulse; GnRH neurons release GnRH roughly every 60 to 120 minutes.
2. Anterior pituitaryGonadotropes carry the GnRH receptor; correctly patterned pulses trigger LH and FSH release.
3. GonadsLH and FSH drive testosterone, estradiol, progesterone, and gamete maturation; sex steroids feed back up and suppress the top two tiers.

Two Peptides, Two Sites

  • Kisspeptin : binds KISS1R on the GnRH neuron itself; the upstream trigger for the pulse generator.
  • Gonadorelin : synthetic copy of GnRH; bypasses the hypothalamus and binds the pituitary GnRH receptor directly.
  • Related outcome (more LH/FSH), two different points of action.

Pattern Over Dose

  • Pulsatile : rest intervals let receptors recover, sustains LH/FSH output.
  • Continuous : no rest interval, receptors downregulate, paradoxical suppression.
  • Belchetz et al. (1978): hourly pulses restored gonadotropin secretion in lesioned monkeys; continuous infusion did not.
  • Long-acting GnRH agonists exploit this deliberately (prostate cancer, endometriosis, precocious puberty).

Evidence Tiers

  • Established : gonadorelin diagnostic test; pulsatile pump therapy for hypogonadotropic hypogonadism.
  • Investigational : kisspeptin for IVF triggering and hypothalamic amenorrhea.
  • Unstudied : either peptide for AAS-related axis support in a self-directed context.

Connection to SERMs (Mechanism Only)

  • SERMs block the estrogen receptor at the hypothalamus and pituitary, the feedback site.
  • Kisspeptin and gonadorelin work by direct excitatory stimulation further along the chain, not by blocking feedback.
  • Both aim at increasing axis output; neither shared goal is a validated recovery protocol.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of how the axis is organized and why pattern controls the outcome, not a license to diagnose, treat, or design a protocol. Absent cycles, suspected hormonal dysfunction, or other alarm signs are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
