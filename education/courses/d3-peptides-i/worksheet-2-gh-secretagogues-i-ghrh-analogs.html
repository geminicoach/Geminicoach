Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of the growth hormone axis, not a license to diagnose, treat, or recommend use, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the mechanism and the evidence work, and treat anything that looks clinical (persistent swelling, joint pain that does not resolve, vision changes, unexplained fatigue, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician.
