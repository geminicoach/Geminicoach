Where education ends
This worksheet builds peptide literacy, not a license to diagnose, treat, or recommend use, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the chemistry and the evidence ladder work, and treat anything that looks clinical (unexplained fatigue, vision changes, persistent swelling, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
