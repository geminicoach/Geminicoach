He wants to understand where each peptide actually acts on the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis, and whether the "more is stronger" claim holds up mechanistically.

Your Tasks

  2. Correct the "more frequent dosing is stronger" claim using the pattern-over-dose mechanism from the chapter, including what happens at the receptor level under continuous stimulation.
  3. Grade the evidence: separate established medical use, investigational fertility research, and the unstudied performance context, and place each peptide's discussed uses into the correct tier.
  4. State clearly where this explanation ends: give no dose, timeline, or protocol, and note what would call for a qualified clinician's involvement.
