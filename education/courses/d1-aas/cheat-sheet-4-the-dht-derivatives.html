Chapter 4 : The DHT Derivatives

One saturated A-ring, five engineering answers, one shared lipid and hepatic bill.

The Second Androgen

5-alpha-reductaseSaturates the C4-C5 double bond in testosterone's A-ring, producing dihydrotestosterone (DHT). Highly active in skin, hair follicles, and the prostate.
DHT vs. testosteroneBinds AR with higher affinity and dissociates more slowly, producing a larger, more persistent signal in the tissue where it is made.
Why it cannot aromatizeAromatase needs the A-ring double bond as its substrate. 5-alpha-reduction removes that double bond, so DHT and every compound built on its scaffold are structurally unavailable to aromatase.

The Family, One Scaffold, Five Answers

Drostanolone (Masteron)2-alpha-methyl added. Injectable ester, not 17-alpha-alkylated. Originally a breast cancer treatment.
Methenolone (Primobolan)1-methylene bond added. Acetate form is orally active without 17-alpha-alkylation; enanthate form is injectable.
Oxandrolone (Anavar)2-oxa ring substitution, plus 17-alpha-alkylation. Oral, hepatic flag applies.
Stanozolol (Winstrol)Fused pyrazole ring, plus 17-alpha-alkylation. Hepatic flag applies regardless of oral or injectable suspension form.
Methasterone (Superdrol)2-alpha and 17-alpha dimethylation. Oral, hepatic flag applies.
"Halodrol"A grey-market product name reused across different, changing formulations over time. Do not assume it maps to one fixed structure.

What "Non-Aromatizing" Does and Does Not Mean

  • Does mean: no meaningful conversion to estradiol, so no estrogen-receptor-driven gynecomastia or fluid retention from this pathway.
  • Does not mean: minimal side effects overall. It is silent on androgenic, lipid, and hepatic risk.
  • The "drying" look traces to the absence of estrogen-linked water retention, not to a diuretic drug action.

The Lipid and Hepatic Bill

  • HDL suppression: class-wide, via hepatic lipase upregulation. Not unique to any one compound.
  • Hepatic risk: tracks 17-alpha-alkylation specifically. Present in oxandrolone, stanozolol, methasterone.
  • No hepatic flag: drostanolone and injectable methenolone are not 17-alpha-alkylated.
  • Pronounced androgenic signature (alopecia, sebaceous/skin effects, prostate exposure) reflects direct skin, scalp, and prostate engagement.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of how the mechanisms work, not a license to diagnose or treat. Abnormal liver enzymes, jaundice, unusual fatigue, or a lipid panel far outside someone's own baseline are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
