Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of how five systems report on systemic risk and why a clinician watches what they watch, not a license to diagnose, treat, or run your own monitoring in place of clinical care. Treat any of the red flags above, or any uncertainty about your own results, as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
