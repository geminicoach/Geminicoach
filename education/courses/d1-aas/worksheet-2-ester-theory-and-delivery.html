Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of pharmacokinetics, not a license to diagnose, treat, or design an injection schedule, for yourself or anyone else. Explain how esters and depots actually behave, and treat anything that looks clinical (signs of infection or injury at an injection site, or any request for a personal dosing or scheduling plan) as belonging to a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose or self-prescribe.
