Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of the aromatization pathway, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the enzyme and the receptor actually work, and treat anything that looks clinical (a new breast lump, a symptom cluster following intentional hormone suppression, or any question about managing an individual's estradiol level) as belonging to a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
