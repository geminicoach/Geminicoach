Where education ends
This worksheet builds biochemical literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the mechanisms work, and treat anything that looks clinical (severe headache, visual changes, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician.
