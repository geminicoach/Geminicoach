Where education ends
This worksheet builds biochemical literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the mechanisms work, and treat anything that looks clinical (abnormal liver enzymes, jaundice, unexplained fatigue, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
