Part C · Why Doubling Multiplies, Not Adds

Where education ends
This worksheet builds reasoning literacy about how risk concentrates and distributes, not a license to design, endorse, or evaluate any specific combination for a specific person. Treat any symptom cluster, or any uncertainty about an individual's own tolerance, as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
