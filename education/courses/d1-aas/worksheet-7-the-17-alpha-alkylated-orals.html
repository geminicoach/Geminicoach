Where education ends
This worksheet builds mechanism literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the modification and its liver and lipid mechanisms work, and treat anything that looks clinical (jaundice, itching, dark urine, persistent symptoms, or abnormal bloodwork) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
