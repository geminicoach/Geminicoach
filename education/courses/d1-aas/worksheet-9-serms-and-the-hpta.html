Where education ends
This worksheet builds mechanism literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how SERM tissue selectivity and HPT axis feedback work, and treat anything that looks clinical (fertility concerns, suspected hypogonadism, or abnormal bloodwork) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
