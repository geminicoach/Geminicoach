Where education ends
This worksheet builds biochemical literacy, not a license to diagnose, treat, or design a regimen, for yourself or anyone else. Explain how the receptor and the rating numbers actually work, and treat anything that looks clinical (new or unexplained masculinizing changes, a request for dosing guidance, or any decision about starting or adjusting hormone therapy) as belonging to a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose or self-prescribe.
