Where education ends
This worksheet builds axis-level mechanism literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the HPT axis and thyroid hormone receptor work, and treat anything that looks clinical (sustained tachycardia, unexplained muscle weakness, suspected axis suppression, or abnormal bloodwork) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
