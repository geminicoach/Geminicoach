Part B · Sequence the Cascade

Number the following events 1 through 6 in the order they actually occur, from receptor binding to the final outcome.

OrderEvent
 Triglycerides are hydrolyzed into free fatty acids and glycerol, released into circulation
 Cyclic AMP activates protein kinase A (PKA)

 The receptor couples to a stimulatory G protein (Gs), which activates adenylate cyclase
 Adenylate cyclase converts ATP into cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cyclic AMP)

Where education ends
This worksheet builds receptor-level mechanism literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the beta-2 cascade works, and treat anything that looks clinical (chest pain, pounding or irregular heartbeat, severe tremor, fainting, significant muscle cramping) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
