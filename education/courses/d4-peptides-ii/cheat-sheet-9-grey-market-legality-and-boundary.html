Chapter 9 : The Grey Market, the Vial, and the Boundary

Mechanism explains what a correctly manufactured molecule does. It says nothing about what is in a specific vial.

The Four Tiers of Oversight

FDA-approved (US)Bremelanotide; full clinical development program and labeled indication.
Approved elsewhereThymosin alpha-1; registered and clinically used in 30-plus other countries, not the US.
Compounded productLicensed pharmacist, 503A (individual patient) or 503B (GMP-inspected facility) pathway.
Research chemicalNo review, no inspection, no pharmacist, no batch record. Where almost everything else sits.

Sterility and Endotoxin

  • Endotoxin survives even after the bacteria that made it are dead.
  • Invisible to the eye; only a validated lab test can detect it.
  • Testing of unregulated peptide vials found detectable endotoxin in every sample checked.
  • A licensed compounding pharmacy can still fail this test, as documented outbreaks have shown.

Identity and Dosing

  • Independent testing found purity as low as a small fraction of the labeled 99 percent claim.
  • The same vials contained more total peptide mass than the label stated.
  • A felt effect does not confirm the vial matched its label.
  • Only laboratory analysis (HPLC, mass spectrometry) can verify content.

The "Research Use Only" Label

  • Shifts legal exposure toward the buyer, not a safety certification.
  • Does not verify sterility, identity, purity, or dose.
  • Legal to sell, legal to use, and safe to use are three separate questions.
  • A seller staying in their own legal lane does not make a buyer's use legal or the vial safe.

Reconstitution and Handling

  • Non-sterile water, reused needles, and improper storage each add risk.
  • Careful technique reduces some risk; it cannot replace lab-based testing.
  • Heavy metal and synthesis-byproduct contamination often produce no symptom at all.
Where education ends   This builds sourcing and manufacturing literacy, not a license to diagnose, verify a specific vial, or recommend a compound. Any adverse symptom following an injection warrants prompt evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
