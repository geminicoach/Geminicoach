Chapter 4 : The Double-Edged Defenders

Two peptides, one blurred border: KPV calms inflammation from inside the cell, LL-37 fights microbes and signals repair from the body's surfaces.

Two Peptides, Two Origins

KPVThe C-terminal tripeptide (Lys-Pro-Val) of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (alpha-MSH). Melanocortin-independent, does not affect pigmentation.
LL-37The mature, 37-amino-acid form of the human cathelicidin antimicrobial peptide, cleaved from precursor hCAP-18 by proteinase 3.

KPV Mechanism

  • Target : nuclear factor kappa B (NF-kB), the master switch for pro-inflammatory gene transcription.
  • Route : acts inside the cell, no melanocortin receptor needed.
  • Effect : lower TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6 transcription, calmer tissue.
  • Claimed uses : gut inflammation; anti-inflammatory ingredient in cosmetic blends (e.g. KLOW-type).

LL-37 Mechanism

  • Structure : amphipathic alpha-helix, one charged face, one hydrophobic face.
  • Direct action : docks on and disrupts negatively charged microbial membranes, kills bacteria, fungi, some enveloped viruses.
  • Also : chemoattractant (via FPR2), cytokine modulator, drives angiogenesis and keratinocyte migration in wound healing.

Evidence Grade

  • KPV, gut inflammation : strong mechanistic plausibility; strongest data is a mouse DSS-colitis, colon-targeted nanoparticle study. No human trial data.
  • LL-37, endogenous biology : extensively characterized in humans.
  • LL-37, self-administered "immune boost" or faster healing : no published human trial establishing a safe, effective dose or route.

LL-37's Double Edge

  • Balanced, regulated release : normal microbial defense and wound healing.
  • Excess or dysregulated : binds self-DNA/RNA, activates plasmacytoid dendritic cells, implicated in psoriasis, lupus, atopic dermatitis, rheumatoid arthritis, atherosclerosis.
  • Same molecule, same core biology. Regulation, not identity, decides the outcome.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of inflammation and host defense, not a license to diagnose or treat. "The body already makes this" describes origin, not safety. A diagnosed inflammatory or autoimmune condition, or any decision to use either peptide, belongs with a qualified clinician.
