Part C · Reason About the Evidence

  1. Khavinson and colleagues found that epithalon exposure was associated with telomerase activity and extended division in cultured human fibroblasts. Explain the distance between that finding and the marketing claim "activates telomerase and extends lifespan."
  2. Explain how ARA-290 avoids the erythropoietic (red-blood-cell-boosting) effects of ordinary EPO while still activating a receptor from the same family.
  3. Humanin's human evidence is described as "correlational, not interventional." Explain what that phrase means, and why a correlation between low humanin levels and aging cannot, by itself, establish that giving someone more humanin would help.

Where education ends
This worksheet builds evidence-grading literacy, not a license to diagnose, prescribe, or recommend a compound to yourself or anyone else. Explain the mechanism and the evidence tier, and treat any request for a personal recommendation or any mention of an existing diagnosis as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
