Chapter 5 : Immune Modulators

Thymosin alpha-1 modulates the immune system's own decision-making. It is not, and never was, a variant of thymosin beta-4.

The Thymic Peptide Family

Thymosin alpha-1 (TA-1)Zadaxin/thymalfasin. 28 amino acids. Toll-like receptors on dendritic cells, T-cell maturation. Approved in several countries for chronic hepatitis; mixed sepsis evidence.
ThymulinZinc-dependent endogenous hormone required for intrathymic T-cell differentiation. Solid physiology, thin exogenous human data.
ThymalinPolypeptide complex from Russian bioregulator research. Real, regionally concentrated evidence, minimal independent international replication.

TA-1 Mechanism

  • No single dedicated receptor identified; interacts with membrane surfaces then engages toll-like receptors (TLR) on dendritic cells.
  • Activates a tolerogenic branch of tryptophan catabolism via indoleamine 2,3-dioxygenase (IDO), supporting immune tolerance, not just stimulation.
  • Promotes CD4/CD8 T-cell maturation and dendritic-cell antigen presentation.
  • Plays out over days to weeks, not minutes.

Sepsis Evidence Ladder

  • ETASS (2013) : 361 patients, 26.0% vs 35.0% mortality, positive signal.
  • TESTS (2025) : 1,106 patients, 23.4% vs 24.1% mortality, not significant.
  • Gu et al. (2025) : pooled benefit did not hold in the highest-quality trials.
  • Lesson: one positive trial is a signal, not a confirmation.

Where education ends   This builds understanding of immune modulation, not a license to diagnose or treat. Being approved elsewhere is not the same as being FDA-approved or a green light for self-directed use. A diagnosed autoimmune or chronic inflammatory condition, or any decision to use an immune-modulating peptide, belongs with a qualified clinician.
