Chapter 6 : Neuro and Cognitive Peptides

Four peptides, four different evidence pictures. "Peptides for the brain" is a marketing pattern, not a shared mechanism.

The Four Compounds

SemaxACTH(4-10) fragment + Pro-Gly-Pro tail. Intended use: focus / nootropic, neuroprotection. Proposed mechanism: BDNF/NGF expression, dopaminergic pathways.
SelankTuftsin fragment + Pro-Gly-Pro tail. Intended use: anxiety. Proposed mechanism: GABAergic tone, sympathetic/parasympathetic balance, enkephalin turnover.
DSIPNonapeptide isolated from rabbit blood, 1970s. Intended use: slow-wave sleep. No defined receptor mechanism.
CerebrolysinManufactured mixture of peptide fragments from porcine brain tissue. Intended use: neuroprotection and recovery after stroke, dementia care. Prescription-only clinical drug where approved.

Evidence Ladder

  • Semax : rodent BDNF/NGF data, decades of unreplicated Russian clinical use.
  • Selank : more advanced Russian regulatory trial history, same replication gap outside Russia.
  • DSIP : small, controlled human studies from the early 1980s, never meaningfully replicated since.
  • Cerebrolysin : largest evidence base of the four, a Cochrane review of 7 RCTs (1,773 participants) found little to no mortality benefit, plus a signal of increased non-fatal serious adverse events at higher doses.

What to Ask of Any Claim

  • What is the stated intended use?
  • What is the proposed mechanism, and is it rodent-derived or human-confirmed?
  • Which rung of the evidence ladder does this specific claim stand on: animal, small/old human, national regulatory trial, or independent systematic review?
  • Has it been independently replicated outside the original research tradition?

What Remains Unknown

  • Long-term safety data in healthy people using any of these four outside a supervised setting is limited or absent.
  • Interaction effects with other medications, including psychiatric medications, have not been systematically studied for semax, selank, or DSIP.
  • Grey-market sourcing outside an approved clinical supply chain carries contamination, purity, and mislabeling risk, independent of mechanism.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of neuro and cognitive peptide claims, not a license to diagnose, treat, or recommend use. Anxiety, attention difficulty, disrupted sleep, and any decision involving a current prescribed medication belong with a qualified clinician.
