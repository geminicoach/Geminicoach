Chapter 1 : One Foundation, Seven Families

One vocabulary, one signaling logic, one evidence ladder, applied honestly across seven very different families.

Three Size Classes

Small moleculeRoughly under 500 daltons; compact enough to survive digestion and be taken as a pill (aspirin, testosterone, metformin). Not built from an amino acid chain.
PeptideRoughly 2 to 50 amino acids linked by peptide bonds; too short to fold like a protein, too structurally specific to behave like a small molecule (GHK-Cu at 3, BPC-157 at 15, thymosin alpha-1 at 28). Usually injected, sprayed, or applied topically.
ProteinRoughly over 5,000 daltons; folds into a stable 3D structure (growth hormone, antibodies, collagen). Almost always injected.

How Peptides Signal

  • Hydrophilic : most peptides cannot cross the fatty cell membrane the way a steroid can.
  • GPCR : G protein-coupled receptor, the largest family of surface receptors; binding triggers a relay in seconds to minutes.
  • Steroid route : diffuses inside the cell, alters gene transcription over days to weeks.
  • Fast at the receptor does not mean fast, or large, at the level of a whole tendon, immune system, or mood.

The Evidence Ladder

  • Top : human randomized controlled trials (RCTs), or a review pooling several.
  • 2nd : smaller human studies, pilot trials, case series, open-label.
  • 3rd : animal / preclinical, rodent, zebrafish, cell-culture data.
  • Bottom : anecdote and vendor marketing, forum posts, testimonials.
  • A mechanism explains what a compound could do. Only a trial shows whether it actually does.

Seven Families, Previewed

1. Tissue repair (Ch.2)BPC-157, thymosin beta-4 (TB-500). Angiogenesis and growth-factor signaling. Evidence: overwhelmingly animal-only.
2. Cosmetic / regenerative (Ch.3)GHK-Cu. Copper-transport and matrix-remodeling signaling. Evidence: modest-to-good topical, thin injectable.
3. Anti-inflammatory / antimicrobial (Ch.4)KPV, LL-37. Calm inflammatory signaling, interfere with microbial membranes.
4. Immune modulators (Ch.5)Thymosin alpha-1, thymulin, thymalin. Thymosin alpha-1 has pooled RCT evidence in sepsis.
5. Neuro / cognitive (Ch.6)Semax, selank, DSIP, cerebrolysin. Neurotrophic and neurotransmitter-tone claims; human evidence thin, small, short-term.
6. Melanocortin (Ch.7)PT-141, melanotan II, setmelanotide. Setmelanotide is FDA-approved for specific genetic obesity syndromes.
7. Longevity / systemic (Ch.8)Epithalon, humanin, ARA-290, VIP. Lowest on the ladder as a group; mostly preclinical.

Key Vocabulary

  • Peptide bond : the link joining amino acids head to tail.
  • Dalton : unit of molecular mass, roughly the mass of one hydrogen atom.
  • Cyclic AMP : a common intracellular second messenger activated by GPCR signaling.
  • Evidence ladder : a consistent way to grade exactly what stands behind a specific claim.

Why This Matters

  • "Peptide" describes size and shape only, never safety, regulatory status, or evidence.
  • The same word covers approved medicine, off-label prescriptions, and unregulated grey-market products.
  • Low evidence is often a statement about who could afford to run the trial, not whether the mechanism is real.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of peptide chemistry, signaling, and evidence grading, not a license to diagnose, treat, or recommend a product. New or unusual physical symptoms, or any individual decision about using a peptide, warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
