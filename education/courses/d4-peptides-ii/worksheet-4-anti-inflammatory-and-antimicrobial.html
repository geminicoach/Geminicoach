  1. What job is KPV actually doing in that blend, and how does it differ from GHK-Cu's role sitting next to it in the same vial?

Part E · Reason About the Double Edge

Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of inflammation and host defense, not a license to diagnose, treat, or recommend use, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the mechanisms work, and treat anything that looks clinical (a diagnosed inflammatory or autoimmune condition, a persistent flare, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
