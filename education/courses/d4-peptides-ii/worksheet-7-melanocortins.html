Where education ends
This worksheet builds melanocortin literacy, not a license to diagnose, prescribe, or recommend a compound to yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (a new or changing pigmented lesion, a cardiovascular concern, or a request for a personal recommendation) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
