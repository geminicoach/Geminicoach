Where education ends
This worksheet builds peptide literacy, not a license to diagnose, treat, or recommend a product, for yourself or anyone else. Explain how the biology and the evidence work, and treat anything that looks clinical, an injection-site reaction, a new symptom, or an existing health condition, as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
