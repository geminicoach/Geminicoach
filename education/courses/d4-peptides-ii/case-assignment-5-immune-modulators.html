The Scenario

Scenario · Callum, 45
Callum coaches a community running group. A friend recently mentioned taking "a thymosin" to help with frequent colds and low energy over the winter. Callum had already read about thymosin beta-4, the peptide behind TB-500, in an earlier part of this course, where it came up in a tendon and tissue-repair context. His first assumption was that his friend's "thymosin" must be a variant of the same compound, maybe aimed at a different tissue rather than a fundamentally different one. Before he responds, Callum wants to sort out what is actually being discussed and how strong the evidence is behind it.

Your Tasks

  1. Explain the structural and mechanistic difference between thymosin alpha-1 and thymosin beta-4: size, target, and what each actually does in the body.
  2. Explain why thymosin alpha-1 sits differently on the evidence ladder than most grey-market peptides, using its regulatory history and the ETASS-versus-TESTS sepsis trial story specifically.
  3. State the intended use of thymosin alpha-1 as an immune modulator, and evaluate whether the evidence supports Callum's friend's specific use, frequent colds and low energy, as strongly as the compound's overall reputation might suggest.
  4. Give a responsible response and state clearly where general education ends here.

Where education ends
You are demonstrating understanding and plain-language evaluation of a claim, not diagnosing anyone or telling Callum's friend what to do. A responsible response explains the mechanism and the evidence honestly, names what if anything would warrant professional input, and does not diagnose. Decisions about using any compound belong with a qualified clinician.
