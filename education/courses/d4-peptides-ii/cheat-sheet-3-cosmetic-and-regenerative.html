Chapter 3 : The Copper Tripeptide

GHK-Cu binds copper and asks skin to rebuild itself: strong at the surface, thin once the claim leaves the skin behind.

The Four-Stage Pathway

1. DeliveryGHK (glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine) chelates a copper (II) ion via its histidine residue, forming GHK-Cu; the complex diffuses into dermal tissue.
2. EngagementGHK-Cu engages copper-dependent enzymes and gene expression in fibroblasts and keratinocytes; chemoattracts macrophages, mast cells, and capillary endothelial cells.
3. SynthesisUpregulates collagen I/III, elastin, and glycosaminoglycan synthesis; rebalances matrix metalloproteinase (MMP) and TIMP activity; releases VEGF and FGF2.
4. OutcomeFirmer, more elastic dermal matrix; new capillary growth; wound closure support.

Copper-Dependent Enzymes

  • Lysyl oxidase : cross-links collagen and elastin fibers.
  • Superoxide dismutase : core antioxidant enzyme.
  • Cytochrome c oxidase : mitochondrial energy chain.
  • Free, unbound copper is reactive; GHK chaperones it into a controlled, useful form.

Topical vs. Injectable Evidence

  • Topical : real controlled human trials on aged skin, firmness, elasticity, fine lines, photodamage. Grade: modest-to-good, with some negative animal findings too.
  • Injectable / systemic : rat ACL-injection study, benefit did not outlast dosing; rodent lung-injury and fibrosis models. Grade: thin, animal-only.
  • Same molecule, different evidence grade by route.

Copper Safety

  • Copper is essential but reactive; the body tightly regulates free copper via albumin and ceruloplasmin.
  • Wilson's disease : genetic inability to export copper, the extreme illustration of overload.
  • Topical GHK-Cu has a reasonably documented skin-tolerance profile at cosmetic concentrations.
  • Injectable and grey-market systemic use has no established human dosing or safety data.

Other Cosmetic Peptides

  • Palmitoyl pentapeptide-4 (Matrixyl) : five-amino-acid procollagen-fragment mimic, palmitic acid tail aids skin penetration.
  • Small randomized human trial data exists (21 participants, crow's-feet area), genuinely encouraging and genuinely limited.
  • Cosmetic "appearance improvement" claims face a lighter regulatory bar than a drug's therapeutic claim.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of copper-peptide chemistry and route-specific evidence grading, not a license to diagnose, treat, or recommend a product. A skin reaction, an injectable product, or any individual decision about using a peptide warrants evaluation by a qualified clinician or dermatologist. Work within your role, whatever it is.
