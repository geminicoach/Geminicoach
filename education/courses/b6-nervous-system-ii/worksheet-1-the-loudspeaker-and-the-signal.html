Where education ends
This worksheet builds understanding of the motor unit and the recruitment ladder, not a license to diagnose, treat, or individually prescribe training, for yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical, such as sharp or persistent pain, sudden weakness, numbness, or an asymmetry that will not resolve with normal training variation, as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
