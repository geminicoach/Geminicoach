Cheat-Sheet · B3 Cortisol

Chapter 13 : Working With the System

Stress management is recovery management. The target is never cortisol itself but the regulation of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis.

The One Reframe: Three Failure Modes, Three Levers

Too-frequent activationThe stressor recurs before recovery completes; peaks stack on peaks. Lever: reduce the frequency and duration of activation.
Failure to shut offThe response fires but negative feedback fails to terminate it. Lever: improve recovery between activations.
Inadequate / flat rhythmThe axis under-responds or the diurnal curve flattens. Lever: restore the diurnal rhythm (morning peak, night trough).
  • Read "chronic stress" as chronically incomplete recovery (McEwen, 1998; Juster et al., 2010). The molecule is not the villain; the failure to let it fall back down is.

Match Intervention to Mechanism

  • Sleep + circadian alignment : anchors the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) clock, restores the diurnal slope (Balbo et al., 2010).
  • Recovered aerobic exercise : steepens the diurnal slope over time; roughly 150 min/week moderate (Ring et al., 2025). Acute rise is the stimulus, not harm.
  • Mindfulness / relaxation : moderate, replicated effect (g approx. 0.34); cortisol awakening response (CAR) moves most (Rogerson et al., 2024).
  • Social support : buffers HPA reactivity upstream.
  • Earlier caffeine cutoff : protects rhythm via sleep; the lever is sleep, not the molecule.

The Buffers (Sapolsky)

  • Predictability : a warning lets the axis stand down in safe intervals; curbs anticipatory firing.
  • Control : perceived control shrinks reactivity, independent of objective control (Trier Social Stress Test study, 2021).
  • Social support : dampened HPA response via oxytocinergic and prefrontal systems (Hostinar et al., 2014).
  • Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) works partly by raising perceived control; these are real inputs, not metaphors.

Reject the Shortcut

  • No "cortisol blocker" : a true blocker is iatrogenic; it reproduces adrenal insufficiency.
  • No "detox / flush" : cortisol is made on demand and cleared in an hour or two; there is no backlog.
  • "Adrenal fatigue" : not a recognized diagnosis; take the symptom seriously and refer.
  • Ask any pitch: which failure mode does this correct? No named lever means a product looking for a symptom.

Educate and Refer

  • Educate : explain physiology, general evidence, correct myths.
  • Never interpret an individual's symptoms as a diagnosis or read clinical testing.
  • Refer : persistent fatigue, mood change, unintended weight change, fainting, salt craving, skin darkening, or any frightening symptom.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of how the HPA axis works, not a license to diagnose or treat. Describe mechanisms and general evidence, recommend only broad low-risk lifestyle domains in general terms, and route persistent, severe, or worrying symptoms to a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
