Part E · Deviations From the Reference

Where education ends
This worksheet builds cortisol literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the rhythm works, and treat anything that looks clinical (a doctor-flagged lab, unexplained weight loss, muscle weakness, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Interpreting a real person's results, including your own, is diagnosis, and diagnosis belongs elsewhere. Work within your role, whatever it is.
