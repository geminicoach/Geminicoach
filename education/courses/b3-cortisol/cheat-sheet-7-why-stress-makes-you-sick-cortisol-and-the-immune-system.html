Cheat-Sheet · B3 Cortisol

Chapter 7 : Why Stress Makes You Sick

One hormone, two faces: acute cortisol redistributes and restrains immunity; chronic cortisol, met by deaf receptors, lets inflammation slip its leash.

Duration Sets the Sign

Acute (minutes to an hour)Leukocyte trafficking redistributes immune cells into skin and mucosa; natural immunity is upregulated. Deployment, not depletion (Dhabhar & McEwen, 1997; Segerstrom & Miller, 2004).
Brief naturalistic (exam week)Cellular, antiviral immunity is suppressed first; humoral immunity is largely spared. Viral defense is the earliest casualty.
Chronic (months to years)Both cellular and humoral immunity are suppressed; the deploy-then-stand-down rhythm is lost (allostatic load applied to immunity).

The Central Paradox

  • Synthetic cortisol is prescribed to shut inflammation down, yet chronic stress is linked to more inflammation.
  • Both are true because the effect depends on how long cortisol acts and whether cells still listen.
  • Resolution: high cortisol plus rising inflammation at the same time, via receptor resistance.

Glucocorticoid Receptor Resistance

  • Glucocorticoid receptor (GR) : cortisol binds it inside an immune cell to switch off pro-inflammatory genes.
  • GCR : under prolonged high cortisol, cells downregulate GR sensitivity and stop answering the "stand down" order.
  • The off-switch is unplugged while the on-switch keeps firing: inflammation rises because cells stopped answering (Miller et al., 2002).
  • The bone-marrow factory may ship out cells that are inherently less responsive (Walsh et al., 2021).

The Two Arms

  • Cellular immunity : T cells and natural killer cells hunt virus-infected and cancerous cells; blunted first.
  • Humoral immunity : B-cell antibodies tag pathogens in blood and at mucosa; suppressed only under sustained stress.
  • Delayed-type hypersensitivity (DTH) : the skin readout that revealed acute enhancement.

Four Places You Can Watch It

  • Infection : blunted antiviral defense raises susceptibility, dose-response (Cohen et al., 1991, 2012).
  • Wound healing : too little inflammation at the cut slows repair (Glaser & Kiecolt-Glaser, 2005).
  • Vaccine response : caregivers mount weaker antibody responses (Kiecolt-Glaser et al., 1996).
  • Latent-virus reactivation : Epstein-Barr and herpes simplex (HSV-1) stir as surveillance slips; rising titres.

Read the Register Honestly

  • The honest claim is association and plausible shared mechanism, not simple single-arrow causation.
  • Chronic stress shifts probabilities and raises susceptibility; it is not a toxin that guarantees illness.
  • Effects are moderate and operate alongside sleep, diet, exercise, age, and genetics. "Nudges" and "raised burden," not "causes" and "guarantees."
Where education ends   This builds understanding of how stress reshapes immunity, not a license to diagnose or treat. Persistent fever, swollen lymph nodes, a wound that will not heal, or unexplained weight loss are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
