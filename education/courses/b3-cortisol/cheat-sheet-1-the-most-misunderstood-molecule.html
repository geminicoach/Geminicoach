Cheat-Sheet · B3 Cortisol

Chapter 1 : The Most Misunderstood Molecule

One idea to carry: cortisol is a regulated signal, not a toxin. Its meaning lives in the pattern, not the amount.

The Four Core Jobs

1. Mobilize glucosePromotes gluconeogenesis in the liver, mobilizes amino and fatty acids, and opposes insulin so fuel stays available. Named for the job: gluco-cortico-id.
2. Tune immunityRestrains the inflammatory response so it does not overshoot. This is why synthetic glucocorticoids are widely prescribed anti-inflammatories.
3. Hold blood pressureSensitizes vessels to catecholamines and supports vascular tone; too little cortisol leads to fainting and shock.
4. Drive wakingSurges in the first 30 to 45 minutes after waking, the cortisol awakening response (CAR), timing alertness and fuel for the day.

Signal, Not Substance

  • Made on demand : built from cholesterol, acts, then cleared by the liver in an hour or two. Nothing to detox.
  • Read by receptors : the glucocorticoid receptor (GR) sets how loudly a tissue hears the message.
  • Fat-soluble steroid : slips into cells and changes gene expression, so effects are broad and durable.
  • Judged by shape : a signal is information; amount is not meaning.

Presence vs Dysregulation

  • Presence : the molecule circulating normally. Healthy.
  • Dysregulation : rhythm flattens, the response fails to switch off, or receptors resist.
  • Same 24-hour total can hide a healthy peak-and-trough or a flat curve. The shape is what a cell reads.
  • "Cortisol is bad" is wrong; "dysregulated cortisol signaling can harm" is accurate.

Acute vs Chronic

  • Acute : the zebra sprints from the lion, then hormones return to baseline within the hour. Protective.
  • Chronic : the human inbox never ends, so the same response is switched on and left on.
  • Allostatic load : the cumulative cost of a response that repeats or never switches off.
  • The variable is duration and shutoff, not the size of any one spike.

The System and the Rhythm

  • HPA axis : the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal loop; CRH and vasopressin drive ACTH, ACTH drives cortisol, cortisol feeds back to restrain itself.
  • Pulsatile + circadian : hourly ultradian pulses ride a daily wave peaking near waking, troughing near midnight.
  • Course arc : molecule, then system, then consequence, then myth-correction, which comes last on purpose.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of how the signal works, not a license to diagnose or treat. "Adrenal fatigue" is not a recognized diagnosis, but genuine adrenal insufficiency is real and dangerous; profound fatigue with weight loss, fainting, or dizziness on standing are signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Understanding the biology makes for better referrals; it never replaces one. Work within your role, whatever it is.
