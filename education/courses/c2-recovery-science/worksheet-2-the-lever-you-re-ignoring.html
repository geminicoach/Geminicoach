Where education ends
This worksheet builds sleep literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the levers work, and treat anything that looks clinical, witnessed breathing pauses, persistent insomnia despite good hygiene, unrefreshing sleep no matter the hours logged, or excessive daytime sleepiness hygiene changes do not touch, as warranting evaluation by a qualified professional. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
