Where education ends
This worksheet builds an accurate model of what recovery modalities actually do, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain the mechanism and the evidence, and treat pain that is sharp, joint-centered, or persists beyond the ordinary soreness window as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician, not an icing or heating protocol. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
