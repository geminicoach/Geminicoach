Where education ends
This worksheet builds training literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the loop works, and treat anything that looks clinical, sharp or persistent pain, a joint that will not move normally, a climbing heart rate trend, or a symptom that outlasts ordinary training fatigue, as warranting evaluation by a qualified professional. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
