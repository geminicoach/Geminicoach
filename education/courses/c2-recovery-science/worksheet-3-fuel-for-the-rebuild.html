Where education ends
This worksheet builds nutrition-for-recovery literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the budgets work, and treat anything that looks clinical, persistent under-fueling, preoccupation with food or body composition, lost menstrual function, or recurrent stress fractures, as warranting evaluation by a qualified professional. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician or dietitian works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
