The Scenario

Scenario · Devon, 38
Consider Devon, twelve weeks into a dedicated hypertrophy block with no competition scheduled for months. After every single strength session he immerses in a cold tub for ten minutes, reasoning that "reducing inflammation is always a good thing," and he follows it most nights with a sauna session because he says it makes him feel "detoxed" and ready for the next day. He has heard that some research questions icing but assumes it must not apply to him, since he genuinely feels better and more prepared for his next session every single time. He asks: "I feel great and recover fast. Why would anyone tell me to stop?"

Your Tasks

  1. Explain, mechanistically, why routine post-strength-session cold water immersion could be working against Devon's hypertrophy goal, referencing what the post-training inflammatory and signaling response actually does.
  2. Evaluate Devon's belief that "reducing inflammation is always good" using the chapter's four-question rubric.
  3. Decide what Devon should do differently given that he is deep in a hypertrophy block with no competition scheduled for months.
  4. Give one practical, general-education suggestion, and state clearly where general education ends and clinical care begins.
