Where education ends
This worksheet builds recovery-science literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the retreat works, and treat anything that looks clinical (pain that persists through rest, unexplained systemic symptoms, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
