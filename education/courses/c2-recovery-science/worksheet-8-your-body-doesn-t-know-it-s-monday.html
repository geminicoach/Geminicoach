Where education ends
This worksheet builds recovery-science literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the shared ledger works, and treat anything that looks clinical (persistent unexplained fatigue, mood disturbance that does not lift, recurrent illness) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
