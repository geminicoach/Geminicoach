Part C · Reason About Convergence and Noise

  2. Why is a single night's HRV reading close to meaningless, while a ten to fourteen day rolling average is genuinely informative?
  3. Explain the "tyranny of the single signal." Why does reading soreness alone lead people astray more than almost any other signal?

Where education ends
This worksheet builds recovery-science literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the dashboard works, and treat anything that looks clinical (sharp or localized pain, illness, or dysfunction that does not resolve with normal recovery) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
