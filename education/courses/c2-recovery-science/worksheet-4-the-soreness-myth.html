Part B · Match the Myth to the Correction

Write the letter of the myth next to the correction the chapter builds against it.

Myths A. Soreness is caused by lactic acid pooling overnight   B. Soreness is a readout of adaptation   C. Soreness grades the quality of a workout   D. Soreness is the same thing as training readiness
LetterThe correction
 Blood lactate returns to baseline within about an hour, long before soreness even begins to build; eccentric exercise produces the most soreness with comparatively little lactate.
 Damas et al. (2016) found that early, high-soreness protein synthesis is mostly repair, not growth; the synthesis that predicts hypertrophy shows up only after damage has attenuated.
 Soreness tracks novelty and eccentric emphasis, not training quality, so a disorganized beginner session can be sorer than a well-periodized advanced one.
 A person can be sore and fully capable of a good session, or pain free and badly under-recovered on other axes such as sleep debt or life stress.

Where education ends
This worksheet builds an accurate model of ordinary training soreness, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the sensation works, and treat anything that breaks the DOMS fingerprint (sharp or joint-centered pain, worsening swelling, marked asymmetry with no clear cause, or pain lingering well past a week) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
