Where education ends
This worksheet builds an accurate model of active recovery, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the tool works, and treat anything that breaks the pattern of ordinary training fatigue (sharp or worsening pain, pain at rest or at night, joint instability, or a cluster of symptoms like unexplained weight loss or prolonged illness) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
