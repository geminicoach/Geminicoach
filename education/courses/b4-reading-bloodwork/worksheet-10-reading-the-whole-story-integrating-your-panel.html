Where education ends
This worksheet builds the literacy to read a full panel as a coherent whole, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Interpretation is literacy; diagnosis and treatment belong to clinicians. A crossed decision limit, a coherent multi-marker pattern that survives its confounders, or anything trending the wrong way across repeated draws over time are signs that call for evaluation by a qualified clinician, not puzzles to resolve alone at a kitchen table.
