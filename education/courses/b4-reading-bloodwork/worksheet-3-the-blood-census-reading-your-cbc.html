Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy in reading a complete blood count (CBC), not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the numbers work and what context moves them, and treat anything that looks clinical (a persistently abnormal line, several disturbed lines at once, or symptoms that accompany the number) as a sign that warrants evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
