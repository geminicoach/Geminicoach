Where education ends
This worksheet builds the skill of reading a lab report as a landscape rather than a checkbox. It is not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Position within a range, life-stage banding, and personal trend are all context that sharpens the questions you bring to a qualified clinician; none of them replace one. Work within your role, whatever it is: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
