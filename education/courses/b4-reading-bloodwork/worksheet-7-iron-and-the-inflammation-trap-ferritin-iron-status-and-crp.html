Where education ends
This worksheet builds iron-panel literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how ferritin, transferrin saturation (TSAT), total iron-binding capacity (TIBC), and C-reactive protein (CRP) fit together, and treat anything that looks clinical, such as a transferrin saturation (TSAT) that stays under 20 percent, a ferritin that will not budge, or an unexplained iron deficiency with no obvious source, as signs that warrant medical evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
