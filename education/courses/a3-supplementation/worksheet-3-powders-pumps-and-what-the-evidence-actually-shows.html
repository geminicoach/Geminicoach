Part E · Caffeine: A Dose, Not a Habit

The evidence treats caffeine as a dose with a response curve, a tolerance story, and a timing problem. Reason through each.

  1. The performance range is about 3 to 6 mg/kg taken roughly an hour before effort. Describe the shape of the curve below the threshold and well above 6 mg/kg. Why is "more is not better" here for a different reason than it is with protein?
  2. Caffeine has a half-life of roughly five hours. Estimate how much of a 4 p.m. dose is still circulating at 9 p.m., and explain in plain language why an afternoon coffee can be a loan against that night's sleep rather than a free boost.
  3. Explain how tolerance turns a daily caffeine habit into a loop where a person chases the alertness they keep undermining.

Where education ends
This worksheet builds supplement literacy, not a license to diagnose, prescribe, or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the compounds work and what the evidence earns, and treat anything that looks clinical (a diagnosed condition, medication interactions, chest pain, racing heart, severe anxiety, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
