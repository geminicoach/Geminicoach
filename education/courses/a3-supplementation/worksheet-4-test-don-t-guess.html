Where education ends
This worksheet builds testing literacy, not a license to diagnose, treat, or prescribe, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the reasoning works and where a gap or an excess is plausible, but ordering and interpreting lab tests and setting a dose from a result belong to a qualified clinician. Treat anything that looks clinical (a flagged or abnormal result, neurological signs, symptoms of toxicity, an existing medical condition, pregnancy, or medication interactions) as warranting evaluation. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose or self-prescribe.
