Chapter 1: Reason Through a Real Complaint

Take the two-job view of food and use it to explain a real complaint, in plain language, within the bounds of general education.

Name: ____________________________ Date: __________ Est. time: 20 min

The Scenario

Scenario · Ben, 34
Consider Ben, who works a desk job and decided to lose the weight he had put on over a few years. He did the obvious thing: he ate less. Breakfast became black coffee and a slice of toast, lunch a small salad, dinner whatever was light, and he skipped the gym because he was tired. After ten weeks the scale was down nine pounds. But in the mirror he looks smaller and softer, his arms have lost their shape, he is cold all the time, foggy by mid-afternoon, and his strength has fallen off a cliff. Ben is confused: "I hit my calorie goal every week. Why do I look worse than when I started?"

Your Tasks

  1. Explain, mechanically, what happened to Ben's body using the two jobs of food (fuel and building material) and the three fates from the chapter.
  2. Account for why the scale dropped nine pounds while his body composition got worse, and name what he most likely lost besides fat.
  3. Decide whether this reads as the predictable result of under-eating or as something that needs a qualified clinician, and say how you know.
  4. Give one practical, general-education suggestion for Ben, and state clearly where general education ends and clinical care begins.

Where education ends
You are demonstrating understanding and plain-language communication, not diagnosing. A responsible response explains the mechanism, identifies what if anything warrants professional evaluation, and does not diagnose. Diagnosis and treatment belong to a qualified clinician.
Instructor / self-check rubric
  • Explains the two jobs of food and that under-eating starved the building material, not just the fuel.
  • Links protein turnover and the lack of dietary protein to loss of muscle, not only fat.
  • Distinguishes weight from body composition and explains why the scale hid the real change.
  • Offers a practical, general-education fix within scope (enough protein, nutrient-dense food, judge by the trend over weeks).
  • Identifies what if anything warrants professional evaluation, and does not diagnose.
