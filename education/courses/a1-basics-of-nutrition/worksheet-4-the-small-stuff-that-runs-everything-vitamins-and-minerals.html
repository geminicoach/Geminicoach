Where education ends
This worksheet builds micronutrient literacy, not a license to diagnose, prescribe, or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the nutrients work and which everyday foods deliver them, and treat anything clinical (a suspected deficiency, persistent fatigue, or a plan to correct a gap with supplements) as warranting confirmation by testing and the judgment of a qualified clinician. Deficiencies are confirmed with lab work, and supplements are targeted tools with real toxicity risk, not treatments to self-prescribe. Work within your role: a coach educates and refers, a clinician diagnoses and treats within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
