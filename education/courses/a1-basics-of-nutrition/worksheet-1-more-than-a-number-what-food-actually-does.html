Where education ends
This worksheet builds nutrition literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how food works as fuel and building material, and treat anything that looks clinical (rapid unexplained weight loss, dizziness, hair loss, persistent fatigue, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
