Where education ends
This worksheet builds metabolic literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the loop works, and treat anything that looks clinical (a lab value someone is trying to read as a verdict, or classic high-glucose signs such as excessive thirst, frequent urination, unexplained weight loss, blurred vision, or persistent fatigue) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Diabetes and prediabetes are diagnosed by clinicians using validated tests interpreted over time, never by a home device or a single number. Work within your role: teach the mechanism, point to the clinician, and do not self-diagnose from a glucose reading.
