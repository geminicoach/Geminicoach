Where education ends
This worksheet builds thyroid literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. You can read the two-number logic and understand what TSH, T4, and T3 represent, which lets you follow a conversation, ask a sharper question, and recognize when something warrants medical evaluation. It does not let you interpret anyone's labs, name a condition, or recommend starting or stopping a supplement or medication. Thyroid conditions are diagnosed and managed by qualified clinicians against context-specific standards, and "thyroid support" supplements plus self-diagnosis are not benign shortcuts, since blind iodine can harm where intake is already sufficient. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose. Treat a persistently racing or irregular heartbeat, or symptoms that are persistent, worsening, or interfering with daily function, as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician.
