Part F · Apply the Thermostat

Where education ends
This worksheet builds hormone literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (interpreting a person's blood work, naming a condition, or advising someone to start, stop, or change a hormone, supplement, or medication, plus alarm signs such as unexplained fatigue, dramatic weight change, or excessive thirst) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
