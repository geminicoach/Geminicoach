Where education ends
This worksheet builds hormone literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how estradiol works across bone, brain, libido, and joints, and treat anything clinical as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Osteoporosis, menopause, and any decision about hormone therapy are diagnosed and prescribed by clinicians, using tests and judgment that live entirely outside this material. Watch for the signs that a situation has moved past general education: a low-impact fracture, a marked loss of height, or unexplained bone pain. Work within your role, whatever it is: understand the biology, respect the boundary.
