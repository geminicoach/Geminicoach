Where education ends
This worksheet builds appetite-hormone literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (relentless hunger that overwhelms daily life, a pattern of restriction, bingeing, or purging, distress around eating that will not lift, unexplained weight change, or disrupted cycles) as signs that warrant evaluation by a qualified clinician. Eating disorders and metabolic conditions are diagnosed and treated by clinicians. Work within your role, whatever it is: refer when a situation leaves ordinary physiology, work within your license if you hold one, and do not self-diagnose.
