Chapter 5: Read the Panel, Not the Marketing

Take the two-timescale stress axis and the "adrenal fatigue" myth apart on a real, worried person, in plain language, within the bounds of general education.

Name: ____________________________ Date: __________ Est. time: 25 min

The Scenario

Scenario · Bianca, 42
Consider Bianca, who runs a small team and has two kids and has felt wrecked for months: exhausted by mid-afternoon, wired at midnight, waking at 4 a.m., craving sugar and salt, and leaning hard on coffee. She ordered a four-point saliva cortisol panel from a wellness website. The report came back with a curve that "did not match the ideal," flagged as "Stage 2 adrenal fatigue," along with a recommended stack of adaptogens, a glandular supplement, and a DHEA capsule. She feels validated that something is finally named, she is about to spend a few hundred dollars a month on the protocol, and she is quietly a little scared. She asks you: "Does this mean my adrenals are burned out?"

Your Tasks

  1. Explain what the "adrenal fatigue" label is claiming, and why the physiology from this chapter (HPA negative feedback, allostatic load) argues against "burned-out glands" as the explanation for her symptoms.
  2. Account for why her saliva "curve" can look "off" without meaning her glands are failing (think about the normal cortisol rhythm, her 4 a.m. wakings, caffeine, and what a single day's panel actually captures).
  3. Bianca's symptoms overlap with a real, diagnosable condition. Name it, explain why that overlap makes the wellness label hazardous, and say what the responsible next step is, including the right kind of test and who orders it.
  4. Give one or two general-education suggestions that stay firmly in scope, address the DHEA capsule specifically, and state clearly where general education ends and clinical care begins. Do not diagnose or counter-diagnose Bianca with anything.

Your Response

0 words
Where education ends
You are demonstrating understanding and plain-language communication, not diagnosing. A responsible response explains the mechanism, identifies what if anything warrants professional evaluation, and does not diagnose. "Adrenal fatigue" is not a medical diagnosis; real adrenal disease such as Addison's disease and Cushing's syndrome is diagnosed by clinicians using specific endocrine testing, and a DHEA capsule is a hormone precursor rather than a benign vitamin. Diagnosis, testing, and treatment belong to a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
Instructor / self-check rubric
  • Identifies that "adrenal fatigue" is not a recognized diagnosis and the saliva-curve panel has no clinical utility for fatigue.
  • Reframes the symptoms as allostatic load / chronic activation rather than gland depletion, using the HPA axis's negative feedback.
  • Explains the cortisol-insulin cross-talk and gets the direction right (elevated or dysregulated signaling, not deficiency).
  • Recognizes the symptom overlap with real adrenal insufficiency / Addison's disease and names the correct clinician test (ACTH stimulation) versus the wellness panel.
  • Treats the DHEA capsule as a hormone precursor with downstream effects, not a benign supplement, and keeps that decision with a clinician.
  • Refers to a clinician for persistent unexplained symptoms and does not diagnose or counter-diagnose.
