Where education ends
This worksheet builds endocrine literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the command chain works, and treat anything that looks clinical (persistent or severe symptoms, a clear change from someone's usual pattern, a lab result to interpret, or anything that feels seriously wrong) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. The tidy two-reading patterns are far messier in real life, where medications, timing, illness, and lab variation blur the picture. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
