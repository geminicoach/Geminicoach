Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about resolution and chronic inflammation, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain the mechanism, and treat anything that looks clinical (a wound that will not heal, a joint that stays inflamed for weeks, or concern about disease risk markers) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
