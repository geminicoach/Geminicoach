Where education ends
This worksheet builds condition literacy, the ability to recognize a pattern, not a license to diagnose or treat yourself or anyone else. Recognizing a pattern is always a prompt to seek professional evaluation, never a basis for self-diagnosis or self-treatment. Glucocorticoids and immune modulators such as thymosin alpha-1 are discussed here only as mechanisms; any decision about using them belongs to a clinician and patient together.
