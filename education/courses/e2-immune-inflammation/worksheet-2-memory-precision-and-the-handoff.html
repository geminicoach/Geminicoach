Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about adaptive defense, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (difficulty breathing, facial swelling, severe or persistent illness, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
