Chapter 4 : Putting Out the Fire

Resolution is not the absence of a signal, it is an active program, and when it fails, the fire smolders instead of going out.

Specialized Pro-Resolving Mediators (SPMs)

LipoxinsMade from arachidonic acid; stop further neutrophil recruitment and begin the switch toward repair.
ResolvinsD-series and E-series, built largely from dietary omega-3 fatty acids; dampen recruitment and promote clearance.
ProtectinsOmega-3-derived; support tissue protection and orderly resolution.
MaresinsOmega-3-derived; drive efferocytosis and tissue regeneration.

Efferocytosis and the Macrophage Switch

  • Efferocytosis : the clean engulfment of dead or dying cells.
  • Engulfing apoptotic cells itself reprograms the macrophage toward repair signals (IL-10, TGF-β) and pro-resolving lipids.
  • M1 macrophage : aggressive, attacking phenotype.
  • M2 macrophage : healing, repair-oriented phenotype.

Acute vs Chronic Inflammation

  • Acute : fast, neutrophil-dominated, self-limiting, protective.
  • Chronic : persists, runs attack and repair simultaneously, produces scarring and dysfunction.
  • The difference is often whether resolution fires, not how the fire started.

Chronic Low-Grade Inflammation

  • A persistent, mostly symptom-free inflammatory state.
  • Linked to atherosclerosis, type 2 diabetes, obesity, and other chronic diseases.
  • Often reflects a deficient resolution program, not just excess activation.

Two Named Routes to Failure

  • Metaflammation : overstretched fat cells recruit inflammatory macrophages and drive insulin resistance.
  • Inflammaging : aging brings slower efferocytosis, lower SPM output, and more senescent cells that secrete inflammatory signals.
Where education ends   This builds understanding of how resolution works and how it can fail, not a license to diagnose or treat. A wound that will not heal, a joint that stays inflamed for weeks, or concern about chronic disease risk markers are matters for a qualified clinician. Work within your role, whatever it is.
