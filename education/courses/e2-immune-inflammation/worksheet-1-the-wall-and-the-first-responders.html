Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about innate defense, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the system works, and treat anything that looks clinical (spreading redness, red streaking, fever, pus, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
