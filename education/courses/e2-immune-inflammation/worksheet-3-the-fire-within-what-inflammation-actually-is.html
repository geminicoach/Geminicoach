Where education ends
This worksheet builds literacy about how inflammation works, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain the mechanism, and treat anything that looks clinical (an unexplained lab result, a joint that is red and hot for days without injury, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
