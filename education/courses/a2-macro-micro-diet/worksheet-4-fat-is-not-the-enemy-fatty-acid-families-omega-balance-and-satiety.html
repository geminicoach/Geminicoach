Part C · The Omega Story

  1. Name the two essential fatty acids and the family each belongs to, then explain what "essential" actually means in nutrition.
  2. Both ground flax and salmon raise omega-3 intake, yet the chapter says you should not treat a spoonful of flax as interchangeable with a serving of salmon. Explain why, using the idea of conversion.
  3. Modern Western diets run near a 15:1 omega-6 to omega-3 ratio while the evolutionary pattern was closer to 1:1. Explain why the reliable fix is adding omega-3 rather than declaring war on omega-6, and mention what happened to mortality risk for both fats in the UK Biobank data.

Where education ends
This worksheet builds nutrition literacy, not a license to diagnose, treat, or manage medication, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how fats and the omega balance work, and treat anything clinical (a diagnosed lipid disorder, medication decisions, persistent symptoms, or a strong family history) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician or registered dietitian. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
