Part E · Engineer the Meal

Where education ends
This worksheet builds nutrition literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how absorption works and how to design better meals, and treat anything that looks clinical (suspected deficiency, persistent fatigue or performance loss, a diagnosed condition, pregnancy, or a decision to supplement iron or other minerals at dose) as warranting evaluation and blood work by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
