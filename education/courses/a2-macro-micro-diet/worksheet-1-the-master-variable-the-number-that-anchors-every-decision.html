Where education ends
This worksheet builds energy-balance literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the budget works and how to read the trend, and treat anything that looks clinical (unexplained weight change, a history of disordered eating, or other alarm signs) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
