Part F · Scope of Practice

Knowing the line between general education and clinical care is a core competency, not a formality. Where education ends
This worksheet builds micronutrient literacy, not a license to diagnose or treat, in yourself or anyone else. Explain how the spectrum works and steer food choices, but treat anything clinical (confirming a deficiency, interpreting blood work, dosing therapeutically, or anything touching pregnancy, medication interactions, or malabsorption) as warranting evaluation by a qualified clinician. Supplements are not treatments. Work within your role: a coach refers, a clinician works within their license, and a self-learner does not self-diagnose.
